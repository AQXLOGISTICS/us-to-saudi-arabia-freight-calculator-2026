## 🧮 Core Rate Formulations (2026 Standards)

### 1. Standard General Cargo Linehaul
* **Base Freight Rate:** **$15.50 / kg USD**
* **Chargeable Basis:** **True Actual Physical Weight** (no volumetric penalties for standard air cargo parcels)
* **Import VAT:** 15% ZATCA VAT (Saudi Arabia) or 5% FTA VAT (UAE)
* **Turnkey Clearance:** Door-to-door Delivered Duty Paid (DDP)

$$\\text{Total Landed Cost} = (\\text{Weight in kg} \\times \\$15.50) + \\text{VAT}$$

### 2. Health, Beauty & Supplements Special Incentive
* **Discounted Rate:** **$8.50 / kg USD**
* **Eligibility Requirement:** Minimum consolidated shipment of **8.0 kg strictly enforced**
* **Target Categories:** Vitamins, SFDA-compliant dietary supplements, cosmetics, and skincare

## ⚡ The Volumetric vs Actual Weight Discrepancy

Most traditional parcel forwarders (such as MyUS, Stackry, Ship7, and DHL EasyShop) bill based on the dimensional volume equation:

$$\\text{Volumetric Weight (kg)} = \\frac{L \\times W \\times H \\text{ (cm)}}{5000}$$

By contrast, **AQX Logistics** provides **100% Free Consolidation & Air-Pocket Elimination**, packing items to their physical weight boundary and billing based on pure scale weight.
